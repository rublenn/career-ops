# Proof Points — Portfolio Case Studies

Source: Akshat Singh portfolio deck ("Social Media & Growth Marketing")

1. **Momo Stall to Shop** (Marketer, Jul–Dec 2020) — Incentive-based referral network drove +67% daily profit growth in 2 weeks (₹1,500 → ₹2,500+/day). No paid channels.
2. **Aphrodite Store** (Founder, Growth Marketing & E-commerce, Jan–Apr 2020) — D2C store cutting out retail middlemen; Meta/Google Ads + WhatsApp conversion; ₹100K profit on 40+ orders in 4 months at ~50% below retail price.
3. **Bottle Company** (Marketer, Aug–Dec 2021) — Social-impact distribution model; drove organic brand recall where customers requested the brand by name at retail shops.
4. **Purvanchal Pannels** (Marketer, Aug 2023–Aug 2024) — Referral network with banks/developers/interior designers; contributed to a 2nd shop opening within 1 year.
5. **Alpheris Medien (India)** (Founder, Sept 2025–Present) — Influencer collaborations + paid ads for nightlife/events launch; sold out nearly every event.
6. **Alpheris Medien (Berlin)** (Marketer, Jun 2025–Present) — Partnered with Uber Eats/Wolt riders for flyer distribution; +1 order/day, alongside Instagram growth. Also grew Alpheris Medien's own Instagram 0 → 900 followers in 2 months and acquired 5 hospitality clients via direct B2B outreach.

## Alpheris Medien Method (agency content system, for interview talking points)

- Discovery: business, problems, audience, aim.
- Monthly content system: 8 Reels + 4 Posts + 1–2 Campaigns.
- Reels are typed by job: awareness (memes), trust (informative), first-visit (interior), repeat-visit (ad/campaign).
- Every reel answers 7 questions before greenlight: vibe, why, who, why it'll work, emotion, offer, shareability.
- Post-launch measurement: engagement, conversion, ROI (CPC/CPA/tables booked), then iterate on acquisition cost, repeat visits, average spend.
