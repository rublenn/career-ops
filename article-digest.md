## 1. Tea Stall Scale-Up (Prabhu's Chai) — peer influence + social proof
*May 2020 – Jan 2021*

- **Problem:** A tiny tea stall with no budget, next to entrenched competitors.
- **Insight:** People trust a peer's casual recommendation far more than any ad.
- **Mechanic:** Recruited local, naturally loud friends to hang out near competitors' stalls. Every ~30 minutes they'd greet the crowd (people they already knew), then casually push: "Come to Prabhu's — the chai's better and you get a free cookie." A small free-cookie incentive sealed it.
- **Result:** Scaled the stall into a full shop within one year.
- **Hero metric:** Stall → full shop in 12 months, near-zero ad spend.

## 2. Rolls Inc (Berlin) — repositioning + rider referral loop + manufactured demand
*Current (Berlin)*

- **Problem:** An Indian fast-food spot promoting "kathi roll" — a term Berliners don't recognize.
- **Play 1 — Reposition on a known category:** Stopped selling "kathi roll." Promoted it as **Indian-style Döner/Duram**, anchoring on a food Berlin already loves and understands.
- **Play 2 — Delivery-rider referral channel:** Gave delivery riders Rolls Inc pamphlets + a personal rider code. Riders hand out flyers on *every* delivery they make (even for other restaurants), turning the city's delivery fleet into a distributed promo network.
- **Play 3 — Manufactured visible demand:** Had riders wait *in front of* the shop while their orders cooked. Passers-by read the cluster of riders as "this place gets tons of orders" → social proof → walk-ins.
- **Status:** Live validation, one of 3 Berlin restaurants Akshat advises.

## 3. CSR Water-Bottle Campaign — ambassadors from goodwill
*Nov 2021 – Feb 2022*

- **Insight:** A campaign that does visible social good earns trust and free word-of-mouth.
- **Mechanic:** Empowered underprivileged individuals as brand ambassadors — they gained dignity/income, the brand gained authentic street-level visibility and public goodwill simultaneously.
- **Hero metric:** Boosted brand visibility and goodwill through a self-reinforcing community mechanic.
