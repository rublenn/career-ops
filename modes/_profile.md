## Proof-Point Story Bank (Akshat's real campaigns)

These are Akshat's actual ground-level neuromarketing plays. Use them in STAR answers, cover letters, and interview prep. (Detailed versions in `article-digest.md`.)

- **Tea Stall (Prabhu's chai):** Used local, naturally-loud friends to greet customers near a competitor's stall every ~30 min and vouch for Prabhu's chai ("better, plus a free cookie") — peer-influence + social proof + a small incentive. Scaled a stall into a full shop in a year.
- **Rolls Inc (Berlin fast food):** (1) Repositioned the product — instead of "kathi roll" (unknown locally) promoted it as **Indian-style Döner/Duram**, anchoring on a category Berliners already know. (2) Delivery-rider referral loop — gave riders Rolls Inc pamphlets + a rider code so they hand out flyers on every delivery anywhere. (3) Manufactured visible demand — had riders sit in front of the shop while waiting for orders, so passers-by perceive high order volume.
- **CSR water-bottle campaign:** Turned underprivileged individuals into brand ambassadors — goodwill + visibility in one socially-responsible mechanic.
