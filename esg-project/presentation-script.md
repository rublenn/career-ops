### SLIDE 5 — The ESG Competency Framework (Applied Visually)

**Headline:** Three-Tier ESG Competency Model

```
TIER 3 — STRATEGIC
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  Sustainability Strategy · ESG Risk Integration          │
│  Board-Level Governance · Stakeholder Engagement         │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

TIER 2 — APPLIED (TECHNICAL)
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  GRI Reporting · CSRD/ESRS Compliance · TCFD             │
│  Materiality Assessment · ESG Data & Metrics             │
│  Supply Chain Due Diligence                              │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

TIER 1 — FOUNDATIONAL
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  ESG Literacy · Framework Awareness (GRI, TCFD, SDGs)    │
│  Corporate Governance Basics · Sustainability Concepts   │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Speaker notes (Akshat):**
> "This is the competency framework we applied throughout our research. Tier 1 is foundational literacy — understanding what ESG is. Most universities, including HS Fresenius, cover this reasonably well. Tier 2 is where the gap opens: applied technical skills like GRI reporting, materiality assessment, and CSRD compliance. Tier 3 is strategic — integrating ESG into business decision-making. Our research found that employers expect graduates to arrive at Tier 2. Most graduates currently leave at Tier 1."

### SLIDE 11 — Case Study 1: Unilever

**Headline:** Unilever: When Universities Don't Deliver, Companies Pay the Price

**Key Points:**
- Unilever's graduate sustainability programme requires all entry-level hires to complete a structured ESG onboarding module (GRI, materiality, Compass strategy)
- Internal data: **64% of graduate hires** required significant ESG reporting upskilling within 6 months of joining (GreenBiz, 2024)
- Unilever HR publicly states preference for universities with integrated ESG reporting training — not just awareness modules
- Cost implication: each graduate requiring remedial ESG training represents an estimated onboarding cost premium of €3,000–5,000 per hire

**Relevance to HS Fresenius:**
> If HS Fresenius closes the reporting gap, its graduates become preferred candidates at companies like Unilever — and alumni become more likely to recommend HS Fresenius to recruiters.

**Speaker notes (Anmol):**
> "Our first case study is Unilever. Unilever is one of the most advanced companies in the world on ESG reporting — they have the infrastructure, the frameworks, and the training budget. And yet, even Unilever reports that 64% of their graduate hires need significant upskilling on ESG reporting within six months. This is not a criticism of graduates — this is evidence that universities across Europe are not delivering the applied skills employers need. The cost is real: Unilever estimates a training cost premium of three to five thousand euros per graduate hire who needs remedial ESG training."

### SLIDE 12 — Case Study 2: Deloitte ESG Talent Report

**Headline:** Deloitte: "ESG Reporting Skills Are Difficult to Find in Graduates"

**Key Points from Deloitte ESG Talent Survey 2024 (n=1,200 professionals, Europe):**
- **58%** of hiring managers rate ESG reporting skills as "difficult to find" in recent graduates
- The report distinguishes between *conceptual ESG understanding* (widely present) and *technical skills* in GRI/CSRD/TCFD (significantly lacking)
- Deloitte explicitly recommends: "Universities should integrate applied ESG reporting modules into core business degrees — not standalone sustainability electives"
- Most in-demand role: **ESG Analyst / Sustainability Reporting Specialist** — requires GRI + CSRD + data skills

**Relevance to HS Fresenius:**
> Deloitte's recommendation maps directly onto our Gap Analysis: the technical Tier 2 skills are the priority gap to close.

**Speaker notes (Anmol):**
> "Our second case study is Deloitte's European ESG talent survey — which surveyed 1,200 hiring professionals across Europe. 58% said ESG reporting skills are difficult to find in recent graduates. Critically, Deloitte distinguishes between understanding ESG conceptually — which graduates do have — and being able to actually produce or interpret a GRI report or CSRD compliance document. That second part is missing. And Deloitte's recommendation is direct: integrate applied reporting modules into core business degrees. That is exactly what our gap analysis recommends for HS Fresenius."

### SLIDE 14 — Recommendations for HS Fresenius

**Headline:** 5 Prioritised, Feasibility-Checked Recommendations

| Priority | Recommendation | Gap Addressed | Feasibility |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Introduce a dedicated ESG Reporting module (GRI, CSRD, TCFD) | -2.7 (Reporting) | High |
| 2 | Embed ESG data exercises in existing Analytics/Research modules | -2.2 (Data) | High |
| 3 | Add double materiality assessment to Strategy or Finance modules | -2.4 (Materiality) | Medium |
| 4 | Create an industry ESG case study bank with corporate partners | Cross-cutting | Medium |
| 5 | Establish annual alumni ESG skills audit to track alignment | Long-term | Low |

**Speaker notes (Anmol):**
> "We have five recommendations, ordered by priority and feasibility. Recommendation 1 is the most urgent: a dedicated ESG Reporting module. This directly addresses the largest gap we identified and requires no structural change to the programme — it is an additive module. Recommendation 2 is even easier to implement: embedding ESG data exercises into existing modules costs very little and addresses the second-largest gap. Recommendations 3 and 4 are medium-term. And Recommendation 5 — an annual alumni ESG audit — is something HS Fresenius could institutionalise to ensure the curriculum stays aligned as ESG regulation continues to evolve."

### SLIDE 15 — Conclusion

**Headline:** The Gap Is Real, Closable, and Urgent

**Three key takeaways:**
1. Employers expect **Tier 2 applied ESG skills** from HS Fresenius graduates — not just ESG awareness
2. The current curriculum delivers well on **strategic and communication** competencies but has a structural gap in **technical reporting and data skills** (average gap: -2.4 points)
3. Two global firms (Unilever, Deloitte) confirm this is a cross-industry, cross-country issue — and both point to curriculum reform as the solution

**Impact for HS Fresenius:**
> Closing these gaps would directly improve graduate employability in ESG-facing roles and position HS Fresenius ahead of peer institutions that have not yet acted.

**Speaker notes (Anmol):**
> "To conclude: the gap between what employers expect and what HS Fresenius currently delivers is real, measurable, and significant — concentrated in technical reporting and data skills. The good news is that it is closable. Our five recommendations are practical and evidence-based. And the urgency is genuine: the EU CSRD has already expanded reporting requirements across European companies, meaning ESG reporting demand will only increase. HS Fresenius has an opportunity to act ahead of the curve and give its graduates a genuine competitive advantage."

## POSSIBLE Q&A QUESTIONS — Prep These

**Q: How did you score curriculum coverage — isn't that subjective?**
> "We used a structured rubric: 0 = not covered, 1–2 = mentioned briefly, 3 = covered in one module, 4 = covered across multiple modules, 5 = applied and assessed. Two reviewers scored independently and averaged. We acknowledge this is a limitation, but the scoring was applied consistently across all 8 competencies."

**Q: Why only 47 alumni — is that statistically significant?**
> "47 responses across a range of graduation years and industries provides sufficient data for pattern identification in an exploratory study. We do not claim statistical generalisability — we present directional findings triangulated with job postings and interviews. A larger longitudinal survey would strengthen this research."

**Q: Your recommendations cost money — is this realistic for HS Fresenius?**
> "Recommendations 1 and 2 can be implemented at low cost: a new module can be built using existing GRI and CSRD public documentation, and embedding ESG exercises into existing modules requires only content updates, not new faculty. Recommendation 3 onward has higher implementation costs, which is why we scored them as medium or low feasibility."

**Q: How does HS Fresenius compare to peer institutions?**
> "We did not conduct a formal benchmarking comparison in this study — that would be a natural extension. However, the Deloitte (2024) data suggests this gap is not unique to HS Fresenius: it is a pan-European challenge, which means HS Fresenius could differentiate itself by acting early."
